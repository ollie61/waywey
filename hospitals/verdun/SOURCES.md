## Known transition conflict

Some specialty pages still mention the former external-clinic counter at M1-252 (Mauve Block), while newer specialty pages explicitly direct patients to the new Black Block at N1 612. WayWey treats M1-252 as transition/verification information rather than silently preferring it.

## Routing policy

- Door 26 is treated as direct access to Black Block N1.
- Current official Accueil clinique instructions explicitly describe the public corridor from the Emergency side through Yellow Block J1 to Black Block N1; that link is encoded.
- The modernization project states that the new pavilion walkway connects to the existing building at every level, but specific public routes to all older blocks have not yet been verified and are not inferred.
- Cross-block routing to Mauve, Green, B and Orange is deliberately withheld until audited.
