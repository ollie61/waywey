# Hadassah Ein Kerem — WayWey dataset sources

Initial package created 2026-10-27. The package is intentionally conservative and is not yet a completeness claim.

## Campus scale

Hadassah's official Ein Kerem page describes an approximately 800-bed tertiary hospital with more than 130 departments and clinics and more than 28 buildings, including the Sharett Institute of Oncology, Mother and Child Center, Emergency Medicine Center and Sarah Wetsman Davidson Hospital Tower.

## Known conflicts / Verify records

- Neurology: the current department page lists Davidson Building, Floor 9. An older patient-information page states the 6th floor. WayWey uses Floor 9 but marks the record Verify until current signage is independently confirmed.
- Blood Bank: official information says ground floor at Ein Kerem but does not identify the building.
- Chagall Synagogue: official information says the middle floor near the elevators but does not clearly identify the building.

## Routing policy

The only initial cross-building public route encoded is Main Entrance ↔ Mother and Child Building. Hadassah's obstetrics directions explicitly state that after security at the main entrance, visitors turn right toward the Mother and Child Building. Other cross-building routes are withheld until validated from a current campus map or equivalent official wayfinding source.
